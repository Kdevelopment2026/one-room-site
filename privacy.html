One Room privacy policy

Last updated 2 October 2026

One Room is an iPhone app for tidying one small area of a room in a short, timed session. It is built so that there is nothing to collect. This page says what that means in practice.

What One Room collects

Nothing. One Room has no account, no sign-in, no server and makes no network requests. The App Store privacy label for One Room is Data Not Collected.

What stays on your device

Your reset sessions (when they started and ended, which area, how many actions you marked done), which tasks are done in each area, any tasks you add or hide, and your preferences (session length, haptics and reminder choices) are stored only on your iPhone, in the app's own storage. They are included in your device backups if you have backups turned on. The developer cannot see any of it.

Camera and photos

One Room does not use the camera or your photo library. The miniature room is a built-in illustration of a typical bedroom, not a scan or picture of yours.

Notifications

If you switch on the evening reminder, One Room asks iOS for permission and schedules a single local notification on the device. No push notification service is involved and nothing leaves your phone. Declining permission leaves every other feature working.

Analytics, advertising and tracking

None. One Room contains no analytics, advertising or tracking code and no third-party software development kits.

Health

One Room does not read or write health data and makes no medical, diagnostic or treatment claims.

Deleting your data

Swipe a session in History to delete it, use History → "Delete all history", or Settings → "Reset room and delete history". Deleting the app removes everything.

Children

One Room does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children, because it collects no information at all.

Changes

If a future version ever adds a feature that sends data anywhere, this policy will be updated first and the change will be clearly described in the app.
