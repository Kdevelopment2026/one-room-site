One Room support

Help for the iPhone app

Getting started

Tap a label in the miniature room (or tap Choose an area for a list), then tap Start 10 minutes. Follow one action at a time with Done or Skip. When the timer ends, tap Stop here, or One more task if you want to carry on.

Common questions

Can I change the session length? Yes. Settings → Reset length offers 2, 5, 10 or 15 minutes.

Can I change the tasks? Yes. Choose an area and tap Edit tasks, or go to Settings → Tasks. You can reorder, hide or add your own tasks, and restore the defaults at any time.

What happens if I close the app during a reset? The reset keeps its timing. When you return, it carries on, or shows the end of the session if the time has passed.

The reminder switched itself off. Notifications for One Room are turned off in iOS Settings. Turn them back on there, then switch the reminder on again. Every reset still works without reminders.

Is the room a picture of my bedroom? No. It is a built-in miniature of a typical bedroom that shows which areas you have reset.

How do I delete my data? Settings → "Reset room and delete history", or delete the app.

Accessibility

Every area can be chosen from a list. One Room supports VoiceOver, Dynamic Type up to the largest sizes, Reduce Motion, Reduce Transparency and Increased Contrast.
